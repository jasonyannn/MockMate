# MockMate Goal

## Product purpose

MockMate helps job seekers turn a specific role and their real experience into focused, evidence-backed interview preparation. It should reduce generic practice, make genuine gaps visible, and help users express their strongest proof clearly.

## Target users

- Job seekers preparing for a known role with a complete job description.
- Candidates who maintain multiple resumes and cover letters for different role types.
- People who want structured written or spoken practice and actionable feedback.
- Users who value a private workspace without connecting Gmail or LinkedIn accounts.

## Core outcomes

1. Match the job description to evidence in the selected resume and supporting material.
2. Identify requirements that are covered, weak, or missing without inventing candidate facts.
3. Produce relevant interview and mastery practice for the selected role.
4. Let users add their own recruiter or hiring-manager questions and receive the same coaching.
5. Build a reusable evidence bank of specific actions, decisions, metrics, and outcomes.
6. Preserve separate, private workspaces for every signed-in user.

## Product principles

- Evidence before confidence.
- Role-specific preparation before generic volume.
- Explain scores and fallbacks rather than presenting uncertain output as fact.
- Keep resumes and cover letters reusable independently, with optional application kits.
- Make the main workflow accessible by keyboard, touch, screen reader, writing, and speech.

## Current scope

MockMate includes account-based private workspaces, role and document management, candidate analysis, interview practice, custom questions, answer coaching, mastery quizzes, an evidence bank, and readiness tracking.

## Non-goals

- Making hiring decisions or predicting whether a candidate will be hired.
- Scraping private social-network data or connecting personal email accounts.
- Inventing experience, qualifications, metrics, or company facts.
- Replacing professional career, legal, or accessibility advice.

## Success measures

- Users can complete the six-stage preparation workflow without losing work.
- Every saved job, document, answer, and custom question remains isolated to its owner.
- Practice questions demonstrably reflect the supplied role or are clearly labelled as a fallback.
- Feedback identifies relevance, structure, evidence, and concision improvements.
- Critical actions meet readable contrast, focus, keyboard, and responsive-layout expectations.
